Modern Conveniences in Pop Culture from the decade immediately after WWII

Yes, we actually made a website.

By Oak, Vavara, Jasper and Kelsie
Modern conveniences in pop culture are things that have been popular in culture and are relevant to a gain in convenience. We are talking about things that were specifically popular in 1945-1955.

ELECTRICAL APPLIANCES,
Post WW2, electrical appliances became extremely popular. Things like cooking, doing laundry, hygiene, vacuum cleaners and much more things were revolutionised. Labour saving devices helped people do things faster around their house and keep everything simple.

KITCHEN APPLIANCES,
Kitchen appliances were small, to emphasise practical use of space, as well as the wars housing constraints. electrical appliances were limited, with like simple refridgerators, gas or coal stoves, and sinks.

HYGIENE,
Laundry machines and dryers became super popular because it made washing clothes and bedding easier, which allowed people to do it more often.

ELECTRONIC DEVICES,
Companies broadcasted many things on radios, which were super popular. Television sets were already being sold.

BALLPOINT PENS,
Ballpoint Pens were invented in 1888, the modern version of which was first patented in 1938, but they only gained popularity in the late 1940s and the early 1950s. This new pen that didnt have to be dipped in ink completely crashed the market for steel nibbed pens very quickly.
💡 FUN FACT
When Gimbels,a department store in New York, first sold ballpoint pens in 1945, they cost $12.50 each. This would be over $180 today. They also sold 10,000 pens on the first day.


POST WAR DECADE TIMELINE (1945-1955)
1945 - WWII ends. Consumer factories stop making military gear and start building household appliances.
1947 - Mass production of home appliances begins to appear across suburbs.
1950 - Television sets start to appear in living rooms.
1953 - Ballpoint pens become most common pen type in schools and offices, replacing fountain pens.
